# MIT OCW Computer Science Curriculum — 50-Minute Talk Checklist

Source: [MIT OCW search — Electrical Engineering and Computer Science × "computer science"](https://ocw.mit.edu/search/?d=Electrical%20Engineering%20and%20Computer%20Science&q=computer%20science)

**A note on this one, unlike Amherst/BGSU/Purdue:** MIT OCW isn't a curated curriculum page — it's a search index with 154 hits, most of them repeat semester offerings of the same course (three copies of 6.046J, three of 6.006, etc.) plus plenty of pure EE hardware/circuits/controls courses that share the department tag without being "computer science" in the sense the other three schools' lists were. I filtered the raw search results down to 69 distinct CS-focused courses (31 undergraduate, 38 graduate): one representative semester per course number (generally the most recent with a usable syllabus), and excluding circuits/electromagnetics/power/control-theory/medical/music courses that only matched by department or incidental topic tag. Each course heading below links to the exact syllabus page fetched to build its talks.
