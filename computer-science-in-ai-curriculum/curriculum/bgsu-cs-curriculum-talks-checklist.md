# BGSU CS Model Curriculum — 50-Minute Talk Checklist

Source: [BGSU Model Course Syllabi](https://www.bgsu.edu/arts-and-sciences/computer-science/model-course-syllabi.html) (Computer Science + Software Engineering, undergraduate and graduate)

Each item is a standalone 50-minute talk, derived from the topics/modules stated on that course's own syllabus page (fetched individually — not just the catalog listing). Graduate courses that pair with an undergraduate course of the same subject (e.g. CS 4120 / CS 5120) are listed separately since their syllabi differ in depth and sometimes add a research component.

> **Note on CS 2310:** the catalog listing shows "CS 2310: Technical Skills for the Digital World," but the live syllabus page at `cs2310.html` describes a course titled "Cybersecurity in Action." Talks below follow the syllabus page. Worth a quick manual check in case the department renumbered/repurposed the course and the catalog list is stale.
