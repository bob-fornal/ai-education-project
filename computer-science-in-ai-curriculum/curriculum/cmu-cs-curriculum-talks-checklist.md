Every course below has talks derived from its actual stated description. Independent-study, thesis, practicum, and "special topics — content varies" courses are marked as having no fixed content rather than given invented talks.

**Total: ~1,768 talks across 574 distinct course numbers** (577 catalog entries — a few course numbers, like 02-120/02-201 "Programming for Scientists," appear twice in the catalog under separate section listings for different terms). This is the largest of the seven schools covered by roughly 3x. Given the scale, the same "no fixed content" flag used throughout this series (independent study, thesis research, practicum, "special topics — content varies") applies to roughly 40 courses here — those are marked rather than given invented talks. Course numbers are reused across the undergraduate/graduate boundary within each department (e.g. 05-410/05-610, 11-661/11-761, 15-410 appearing alongside near-identical graduate-numbered twins) — where a paired course had an identical catalog description, its talks are identical by design, not duplicated in error.
